Kumbuka: Linear search in JavaScript: find the position of a value in a list

Unda namba yenye thamani orodha (31, 41, 59, 26, 53, 58, 97, 93).
Unda wanted yenye thamani 53.
Unda nafasi yenye thamani -1.
Unda sasa yenye thamani 0.
Kwa kila fahirisi kutoka 0 hadi urefu wa namba toa 1, rudia hivi:
  Unda nambari yenye thamani kipengele fahirisi cha namba.
  Ikiwa nambari ni sawa na wanted na nafasi ni chini ya 0, fanya hivi:
    Badilisha nafasi kuwa sasa.
  Badilisha sasa kuwa sasa jumlisha 1.
Andika “Found ” ikifuatiwa na wanted ikifuatiwa na “ at position ” ikifuatiwa na nafasi na umalize mstari.
