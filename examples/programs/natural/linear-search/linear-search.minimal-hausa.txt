Lura: Linear search in JavaScript: find the position of a value in a list

Akwai adadai, shi ne waɗannan abubuwa (31, 41, 59, 26, 53, 58, 97, 93).
Akwai wanted, shi ne 53.
Akwai matsayi, shi ne -1.
Akwai yanzu, shi ne 0.
Ga kowane fihirisa daga 0 zuwa yawan sassan adadai ragi 1, yi wannan:
  Akwai lamba, shi ne sashe fihirisa na adadai.
  Idan lamba daidai yake da wanted kuma matsayi ya gaza 0, yi wannan:
    Bayan wannan, matsayi ya zama yanzu.
  Bayan wannan, yanzu ya zama yanzu ƙari 1.
Faɗi “Found ” sannan wanted sannan “ at position ” sannan matsayi a layi ɗaya.
