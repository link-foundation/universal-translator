Note: Towers of Hanoi in JavaScript, solved recursively

Make move-disks from count, source, target and spare by these steps:
  If count is 0, do this:
    Stop here.
  Do move-disks of (count minus 1, source, spare, target).
  Say “Move disk ” then count then “ from ” then source then “ to ” then target and end the line.
  Do move-disks of (count minus 1, spare, target, source).

Do move-disks of (4, 1, 3, 2).
