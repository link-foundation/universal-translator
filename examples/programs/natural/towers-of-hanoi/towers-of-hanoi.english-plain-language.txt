Note: Towers of Hanoi in JavaScript, solved recursively

Here is how to work out move-disks from count, source, target and spare:
  If count is 0, do this:
    Stop here.
  Run move-disks of (count minus 1, source, spare, target).
  Show “Move disk ” then count then “ from ” then source then “ to ” then target on its own line.
  Run move-disks of (count minus 1, spare, target, source).

Run move-disks of (4, 1, 3, 2).
