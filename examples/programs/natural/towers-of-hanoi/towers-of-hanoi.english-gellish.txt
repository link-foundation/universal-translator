Note: Towers of Hanoi in JavaScript, solved recursively

Move-disks is defined as a procedure with input count, source, target and spare:
  If count is equal to 0 then the following is performed:
    The procedure is ended.
  The action move-disks applied to (count minus 1, source, spare, target) is performed.
  The output has as next line “Move disk ” concatenated with count concatenated with “ from ” concatenated with source concatenated with “ to ” concatenated with target.
  The action move-disks applied to (count minus 1, spare, target, source) is performed.

The action move-disks applied to (4, 1, 3, 2) is performed.
