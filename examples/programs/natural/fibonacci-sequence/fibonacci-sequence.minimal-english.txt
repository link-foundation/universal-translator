Note: Fibonacci sequence in JavaScript, computed iteratively

This is how someone does fibonacci with count:
  There is previous, it is 0.
  There is current, it is 1.
  For all step from 0 to count minus 1, do this:
    There is next, it is previous plus current.
    After this, previous is current.
    After this, current is next.
  The answer is previous.

For all index from 0 to 20, do this:
  Say “fibonacci(” then index then “) = ” then fibonacci of (index) on one line.
