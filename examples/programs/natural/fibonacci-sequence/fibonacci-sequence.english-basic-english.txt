Note: Fibonacci sequence in JavaScript, computed iteratively

Make fibonacci from count by these steps:
  Let previous be 0.
  Let current be 1.
  For every step from 0 till count minus 1, do this:
    Let next be previous plus current.
    Let previous now be current.
    Let current now be next.
  Give back previous.

For every index from 0 till 20, do this:
  Say “fibonacci(” then index then “) = ” then fibonacci of (index) and end the line.
