Note: Fibonacci sequence in JavaScript, computed iteratively

To get fibonacci from count, do this:
  Let previous be 0.
  Let current be 1.
  For each step from 0 to count minus 1, do this:
    Let next be previous plus current.
    Now let previous be current.
    Now let current be next.
  Return previous.

For each index from 0 to 20, do this:
  Say “fibonacci(” then index then “) = ” then fibonacci of (index) on a line.
