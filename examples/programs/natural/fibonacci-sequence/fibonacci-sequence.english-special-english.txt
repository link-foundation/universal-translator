Note: Fibonacci sequence in JavaScript, computed iteratively

Here is how to find fibonacci from count:
  Start previous at 0.
  Start current at 1.
  For each step from 0 to count minus 1, do this:
    Start next at previous plus current.
    Change previous to current.
    Change current to next.
  Return previous.
