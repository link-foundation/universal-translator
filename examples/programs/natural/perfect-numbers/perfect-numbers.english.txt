Note: Perfect numbers in JavaScript: numbers equal to the sum of their divisors

Define is-perfect using number:
  Create sum with value 1.
  Create divisor with value 2.
  While divisor times divisor does not exceed number, repeat this:
    If number modulo divisor equals 0, do this:
      Change sum to sum plus divisor.
      If divisor times divisor does not equal number, do this:
        Change sum to sum plus number divided by divisor.
    Change divisor to divisor plus 1.
  Give back number exceeds 1 and sum equals number.

For each number from 1 to 10000, repeat this:
  If is-perfect of (number), do this:
    Write number and end the line.
