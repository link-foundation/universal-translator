Lura: Perfect numbers in JavaScript: numbers equal to the sum of their divisors

Ayyana shin-cikakke da lamba:
  Ƙirƙiri jimla mai ƙima 1.
  Ƙirƙiri rabo mai ƙima 2.
  Yayin da rabo sau rabo bai wuce lamba, maimaita wannan:
    Idan lamba modulo rabo daidai yake da 0, yi wannan:
      Canza jimla zuwa jimla ƙari rabo.
      Idan rabo sau rabo bai yi daidai da lamba, yi wannan:
        Canza jimla zuwa jimla ƙari lamba raba da rabo.
    Canza rabo zuwa rabo ƙari 1.
  Mayar da lamba ya wuce 1 kuma jimla daidai yake da lamba.

Ga kowane lamba daga 1 zuwa 10000, maimaita wannan:
  Idan shin-cikakke na (lamba), yi wannan:
    Rubuta lamba kuma ka gama layi.
