Note: Perfect numbers in JavaScript: numbers equal to the sum of their divisors

Rule is-perfect with number is defined as follows:
  It is obligatory that sum is initially 1.
  It is obligatory that divisor is initially 2.
  As long as divisor times divisor is at most number the following applies repeatedly:
    If number modulo divisor is equal to 0 then the following applies:
      It is obligatory that sum becomes sum plus divisor.
      If divisor times divisor is not equal to number then the following applies:
        It is obligatory that sum becomes sum plus number divided by divisor.
    It is obligatory that divisor becomes divisor plus 1.
  It is obligatory that the result is number is greater than 1 and sum is equal to number.

For each number from 1 to 10000 the following applies:
  If is-perfect of (number) then the following applies:
    It is obligatory that the output shows number as a line.
