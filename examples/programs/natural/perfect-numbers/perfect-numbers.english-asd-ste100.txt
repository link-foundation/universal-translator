Note: Perfect numbers in JavaScript: numbers equal to the sum of their divisors

Procedure is-perfect with number:
  Set sum to 1.
  Set divisor to 2.
  While divisor times divisor is not more than number, do these steps again:
    If number modulo divisor equals 0, do these steps:
      Change sum to sum plus divisor.
      If divisor times divisor is not number, do these steps:
        Change sum to sum plus number divided by divisor.
    Change divisor to divisor plus 1.
  Return number is more than 1 and sum equals number.

For each number from 1 to 10000, do these steps:
  If is-perfect of (number), do these steps:
    Write line number.
