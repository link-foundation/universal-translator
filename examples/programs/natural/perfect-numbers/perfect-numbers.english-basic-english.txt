Note: Perfect numbers in JavaScript: numbers equal to the sum of their divisors

Make is-perfect from number by these steps:
  Let sum be 1.
  Let divisor be 2.
  While divisor times divisor is not over number, do this again and again:
    If number modulo divisor is 0, do this:
      Let sum now be sum plus divisor.
      If divisor times divisor is not number, do this:
        Let sum now be sum plus number divided by divisor.
    Let divisor now be divisor plus 1.
  Give back number is over 1 and sum is number.

For every number from 1 till 10000, do this:
  If is-perfect of (number), do this:
    Say number and end the line.
