Lura: Perfect numbers in JavaScript: numbers equal to the sum of their divisors

Haka wani yake yin shin-cikakke da lamba:
  Akwai jimla, shi ne 1.
  Akwai rabo, shi ne 2.
  Lokacin da rabo sau rabo bai fi lamba ba, yi wannan sau da yawa:
    Idan lamba modulo rabo daidai yake da 0, yi wannan:
      Bayan wannan, jimla ya zama jimla ƙari rabo.
      Idan rabo sau rabo bai yi daidai da lamba ba, yi wannan:
        Bayan wannan, jimla ya zama jimla ƙari lamba raba da rabo.
    Bayan wannan, rabo ya zama rabo ƙari 1.
  Amsar ita ce lamba ya fi 1 kuma jimla daidai yake da lamba.

Ga kowane lamba daga 1 zuwa 10000, yi wannan:
  Idan shin-cikakke na (lamba), yi wannan:
    Faɗi lamba a layi ɗaya.
