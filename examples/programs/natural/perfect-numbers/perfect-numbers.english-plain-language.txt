Note: Perfect numbers in JavaScript: numbers equal to the sum of their divisors

Here is how to work out is-perfect from number:
  Let sum start as 1.
  Let divisor start as 2.
  As long as divisor times divisor is at most number, keep doing this:
    If number modulo divisor is 0, do this:
      Set sum to sum plus divisor.
      If divisor times divisor is not number, do this:
        Set sum to sum plus number divided by divisor.
    Set divisor to divisor plus 1.
  The answer is number is more than 1 and sum is number.

For each number from 1 to 10000, do this:
  If is-perfect of (number), do this:
    Show number on its own line.
