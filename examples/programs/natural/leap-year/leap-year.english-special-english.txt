Note: Leap year in JavaScript

Here is how to find is-leap-year from year:
  Return year modulo 4 equals 0 and year modulo 100 is not 0 or year modulo 400 equals 0.

For each year from 1896 to 1912, do this:
  If is-leap-year of (year), do this:
    Show year then “ is a leap year” on a line.
  If not, do this:
    Show year then “ is not a leap year” on a line.
