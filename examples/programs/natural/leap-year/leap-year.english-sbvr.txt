Note: Leap year in JavaScript

Rule is-leap-year with year is defined as follows:
  It is obligatory that the result is year modulo 4 is equal to 0 and year modulo 100 is not equal to 0 or year modulo 400 is equal to 0.

For each year from 1896 to 1912 the following applies:
  If is-leap-year of (year) then the following applies:
    It is obligatory that the output shows year followed by “ is a leap year” as a line.
  Otherwise the following applies:
    It is obligatory that the output shows year followed by “ is not a leap year” as a line.
