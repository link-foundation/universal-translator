Note: Leap year in JavaScript

Define is-leap-year using year:
  Give back year modulo 4 equals 0 and year modulo 100 does not equal 0 or year modulo 400 equals 0.

For each year from 1896 to 1912, repeat this:
  If is-leap-year of (year), do this:
    Write year followed by “ is a leap year” and end the line.
  Otherwise, do this:
    Write year followed by “ is not a leap year” and end the line.
