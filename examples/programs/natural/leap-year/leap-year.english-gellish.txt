Note: Leap year in JavaScript

Is-leap-year is defined as a procedure with input year:
  The procedure has as result year modulo 4 is equal to 0 and year modulo 100 is not equal to 0 or year modulo 400 is equal to 0.

For each year from 1896 to 1912 the following is repeated:
  If is-leap-year applied to (year) then the following is performed:
    The output has as next line year concatenated with “ is a leap year”.
  Else the following is performed:
    The output has as next line year concatenated with “ is not a leap year”.
