Lura: Leap year in JavaScript

Ayyana shin-tsalle-shekara da shekara:
  Mayar da shekara modulo 4 daidai yake da 0 kuma shekara modulo 100 bai yi daidai da 0 ko shekara modulo 400 daidai yake da 0.

Ga kowane shekara daga 1896 zuwa 1912, maimaita wannan:
  Idan shin-tsalle-shekara na (shekara), yi wannan:
    Rubuta shekara sannan “ is a leap year” kuma ka gama layi.
  In ba haka ba, yi wannan:
    Rubuta shekara sannan “ is not a leap year” kuma ka gama layi.
