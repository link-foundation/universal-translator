Note: Sum multiples of 3 and 5 in JavaScript: the sum of the multiples below 1000

Total is classified as a variable with initial value 0.
For each number from 1 to 999 the following is repeated:
  If number modulo 3 is equal to 0 or number modulo 5 is equal to 0 then the following is performed:
    Total has as new value total plus number.
The output has as next line total.
