Note: Primality by trial division in JavaScript

Make is-prime from number by these steps:
  If number is under 2, do this:
    Give back false.
  Let divisor be 2.
  While divisor times divisor is not over number, do this again and again:
    If number modulo divisor is 0, do this:
      Give back false.
    Let divisor now be divisor plus 1.
  Give back true.

For every number from 1 till 50, do this:
  If is-prime of (number), do this:
    Say number and end the line.
