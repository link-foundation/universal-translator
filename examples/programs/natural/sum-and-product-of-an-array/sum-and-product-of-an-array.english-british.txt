Note: Sum and product of an array in JavaScript

Create numbers with value the list (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
Create total with value 0.
Create product with value 1.
For each index from 0 to the length of numbers minus 1, repeat this:
  Change total to total plus item index of numbers.
  Change product to product times item index of numbers.
Write ‘Sum: ’ followed by total and end the line.
Write ‘Product: ’ followed by product and end the line.
