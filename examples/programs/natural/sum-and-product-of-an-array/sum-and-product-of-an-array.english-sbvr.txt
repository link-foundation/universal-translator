Note: Sum and product of an array in JavaScript

It is obligatory that numbers is initially the list (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
It is obligatory that total is initially 0.
It is obligatory that product is initially 1.
For each index from 0 to the length of numbers minus 1 the following applies:
  It is obligatory that total becomes total plus item index of numbers.
  It is obligatory that product becomes product times item index of numbers.
It is obligatory that the output shows “Sum: ” followed by total as a line.
It is obligatory that the output shows “Product: ” followed by product as a line.
