Note: Sum and product of an array in JavaScript

There is numbers, it is these things (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
There is total, it is 0.
There is product, it is 1.
For all index from 0 to how many parts numbers has minus 1, do this:
  After this, total is total plus part index of numbers.
  After this, product is product times part index of numbers.
Say “Sum: ” then total on one line.
Say “Product: ” then product on one line.
