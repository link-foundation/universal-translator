Note: Sum and product of an array in JavaScript

Set numbers to the list (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
Set total to 0.
Set product to 1.
For each index from 0 to the length of numbers minus 1, do these steps:
  Change total to total plus item index of numbers.
  Change product to product times item index of numbers.
Write line “Sum: ” then total.
Write line “Product: ” then product.
