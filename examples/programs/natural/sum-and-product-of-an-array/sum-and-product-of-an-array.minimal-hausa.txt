Lura: Sum and product of an array in JavaScript

Akwai adadai, shi ne waɗannan abubuwa (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
Akwai duka, shi ne 0.
Akwai sakamako, shi ne 1.
Ga kowane fihirisa daga 0 zuwa yawan sassan adadai ragi 1, yi wannan:
  Bayan wannan, duka ya zama duka ƙari sashe fihirisa na adadai.
  Bayan wannan, sakamako ya zama sakamako sau sashe fihirisa na adadai.
Faɗi “Sum: ” sannan duka a layi ɗaya.
Faɗi “Product: ” sannan sakamako a layi ɗaya.
