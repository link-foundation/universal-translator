Note: Sum and product of an array in JavaScript

Let numbers be the list (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
Let total be 0.
Let product be 1.
For each index from 0 to the size of numbers minus 1, do this:
  Now let total be total plus part index of numbers.
  Now let product be product times part index of numbers.
Say “Sum: ” then total on a line.
Say “Product: ” then product on a line.
