Note: Sum and product of an array in JavaScript

Let numbers start as the list (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
Let total start as 0.
Let product start as 1.
For each index from 0 to the length of numbers minus 1, do this:
  Set total to total plus item index of numbers.
  Set product to product times item index of numbers.
Show “Sum: ” then total on its own line.
Show “Product: ” then product on its own line.
