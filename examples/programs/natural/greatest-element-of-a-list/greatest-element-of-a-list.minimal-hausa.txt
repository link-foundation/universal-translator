Lura: Greatest element of a list in JavaScript

Akwai adadai, shi ne waɗannan abubuwa (17, 4, 93, 28, 65, 3, 80).
Akwai babba, shi ne sashe 0 na adadai.
Ga kowane fihirisa daga 0 zuwa yawan sassan adadai ragi 1, yi wannan:
  Akwai lamba, shi ne sashe fihirisa na adadai.
  Idan lamba ya fi babba, yi wannan:
    Bayan wannan, babba ya zama lamba.
