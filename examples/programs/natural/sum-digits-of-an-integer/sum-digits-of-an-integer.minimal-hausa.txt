Lura: Sum digits of an integer in JavaScript

Haka wani yake yin jimla-na-lambobi da lamba:
  Akwai duka, shi ne 0.
  Lokacin da lamba ya fi 0, yi wannan sau da yawa:
    Bayan wannan, duka ya zama duka ƙari lamba modulo 10.
    Bayan wannan, lamba ya zama lamba raba da 10.
  Amsar ita ce duka.

Faɗi “The digits of 1234567890 sum to ” sannan jimla-na-lambobi na (1234567890) a layi ɗaya.
