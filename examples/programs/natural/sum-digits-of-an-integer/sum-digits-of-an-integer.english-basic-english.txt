Note: Sum digits of an integer in JavaScript

Make sum-of-digits from number by these steps:
  Let total be 0.
  While number is over 0, do this again and again:
    Let total now be total plus number modulo 10.
    Let number now be number divided by 10.
  Give back total.

Say “The digits of 1234567890 sum to ” then sum-of-digits of (1234567890) and end the line.
