Note: Sum digits of an integer in JavaScript

Define sum-of-digits using number:
  Create total with value 0.
  While number exceeds 0, repeat this:
    Change total to total plus number modulo 10.
    Change number to number divided by 10.
  Give back total.

Write “The digits of 1234567890 sum to ” followed by sum-of-digits of (1234567890) and end the line.
