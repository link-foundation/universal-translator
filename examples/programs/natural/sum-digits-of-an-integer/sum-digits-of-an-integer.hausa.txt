Lura: Sum digits of an integer in JavaScript

Ayyana jimla-na-lambobi da lamba:
  Ƙirƙiri duka mai ƙima 0.
  Yayin da lamba ya wuce 0, maimaita wannan:
    Canza duka zuwa duka ƙari lamba modulo 10.
    Canza lamba zuwa lamba raba da 10.
  Mayar da duka.

Rubuta “The digits of 1234567890 sum to ” sannan jimla-na-lambobi na (1234567890) kuma ka gama layi.
