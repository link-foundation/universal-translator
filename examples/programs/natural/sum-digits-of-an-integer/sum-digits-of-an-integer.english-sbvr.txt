Note: Sum digits of an integer in JavaScript

Rule sum-of-digits with number is defined as follows:
  It is obligatory that total is initially 0.
  As long as number is greater than 0 the following applies repeatedly:
    It is obligatory that total becomes total plus number modulo 10.
    It is obligatory that number becomes number divided by 10.
  It is obligatory that the result is total.

It is obligatory that the output shows “The digits of 1234567890 sum to ” followed by sum-of-digits of (1234567890) as a line.
