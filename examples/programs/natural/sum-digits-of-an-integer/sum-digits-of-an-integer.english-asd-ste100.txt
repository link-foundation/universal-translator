Note: Sum digits of an integer in JavaScript

Procedure sum-of-digits with number:
  Set total to 0.
  While number is more than 0, do these steps again:
    Change total to total plus number modulo 10.
    Change number to number divided by 10.
  Return total.

Write line “The digits of 1234567890 sum to ” then sum-of-digits of (1234567890).
