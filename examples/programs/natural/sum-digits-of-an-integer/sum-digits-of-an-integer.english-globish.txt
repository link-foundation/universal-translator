Note: Sum digits of an integer in JavaScript

To get sum-of-digits from number, do this:
  Let total be 0.
  While number is more than 0, do this again:
    Now let total be total plus number modulo 10.
    Now let number be number divided by 10.
  Return total.

Say “The digits of 1234567890 sum to ” then sum-of-digits of (1234567890) on a line.
