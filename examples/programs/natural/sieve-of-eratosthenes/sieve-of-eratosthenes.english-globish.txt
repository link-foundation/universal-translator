Note: Sieve of Eratosthenes in JavaScript: the primes up to 100

Let limit be 100.
Let is-prime be a list of (limit plus 1) copies of 1.
Now let part 0 of is-prime be 0.
Now let part 1 of is-prime be 0.
For each number from 2 to limit, do this:
  If part number of is-prime equals 1, do this:
    Say number on a line.
    Let multiple be number times number.
    While multiple is not more than limit, do this again:
      Now let part multiple of is-prime be 0.
      Now let multiple be multiple plus number.
