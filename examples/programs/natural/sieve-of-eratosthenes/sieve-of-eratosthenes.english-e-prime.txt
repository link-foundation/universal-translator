Note: Sieve of Eratosthenes in JavaScript: the primes up to 100

Create limit with value 100.
Create check-prime with value a list of (limit plus 1) copies of 1.
Change item 0 of check-prime to 0.
Change item 1 of check-prime to 0.
For each number from 2 to limit, repeat this:
  If item number of check-prime equals 1, do this:
    Write number and end the line.
    Create multiple with value number times number.
    While multiple does not exceed limit, repeat this:
      Change item multiple of check-prime to 0.
      Change multiple to multiple plus number.
