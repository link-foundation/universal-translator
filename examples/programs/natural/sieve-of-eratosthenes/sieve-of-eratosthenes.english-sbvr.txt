Note: Sieve of Eratosthenes in JavaScript: the primes up to 100

It is obligatory that limit is initially 100.
It is obligatory that is-prime is initially a list of (limit plus 1) copies of 1.
It is obligatory that item 0 of is-prime becomes 0.
It is obligatory that item 1 of is-prime becomes 0.
For each number from 2 to limit the following applies:
  If item number of is-prime is equal to 1 then the following applies:
    It is obligatory that the output shows number as a line.
    It is obligatory that multiple is initially number times number.
    As long as multiple is at most limit the following applies repeatedly:
      It is obligatory that item multiple of is-prime becomes 0.
      It is obligatory that multiple becomes multiple plus number.
