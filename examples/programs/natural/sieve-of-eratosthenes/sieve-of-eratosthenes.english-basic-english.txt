Note: Sieve of Eratosthenes in JavaScript: the primes up to 100

Let limit be 100.
Let is-prime be a list of (limit plus 1) copies of 1.
Let part 0 of is-prime now be 0.
Let part 1 of is-prime now be 0.
For every number from 2 till limit, do this:
  If part number of is-prime is 1, do this:
    Say number and end the line.
    Let multiple be number times number.
    While multiple is not over limit, do this again and again:
      Let part multiple of is-prime now be 0.
      Let multiple now be multiple plus number.
