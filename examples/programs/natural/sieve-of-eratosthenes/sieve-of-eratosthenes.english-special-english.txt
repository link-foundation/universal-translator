Note: Sieve of Eratosthenes in JavaScript: the primes up to 100

Start limit at 100.
Start is-prime at a list of (limit plus 1) copies of 1.
Change part 0 of is-prime to 0.
Change part 1 of is-prime to 0.
For each number from 2 to limit, do this:
  If part number of is-prime equals 1, do this:
    Show number on a line.
    Start multiple at number times number.
    While multiple is not more than limit, do this again:
      Change part multiple of is-prime to 0.
      Change multiple to multiple plus number.
