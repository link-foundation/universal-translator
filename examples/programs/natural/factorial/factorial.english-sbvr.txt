Note: Factorial in JavaScript, defined recursively

Rule factorial with number is defined as follows:
  If number is at most 1 then the following applies:
    It is obligatory that the result is 1.
  It is obligatory that the result is number times factorial of (number minus 1).

For each number from 0 to 10 the following applies:
  It is obligatory that the output shows number followed by “! = ” followed by factorial of (number) as a line.
