Note: Mutual recursion in JavaScript: the Hofstadter Female and Male sequences

Make female from index by these steps:
  If index is 0, do this:
    Give back 1.
  Give back index minus male of (female of (index minus 1)).

Make male from index by these steps:
  If index is 0, do this:
    Give back 0.
  Give back index minus female of (male of (index minus 1)).

For every index from 0 till 19, do this:
  Say index then “: ” then female of (index) then “ ” then male of (index) and end the line.
