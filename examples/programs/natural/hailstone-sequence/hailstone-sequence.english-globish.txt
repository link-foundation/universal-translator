Note: Hailstone sequence in JavaScript: count the steps to reach 1

To get hailstone-steps from number, do this:
  Let steps be 0.
  While number is not 1, do this again:
    If number modulo 2 equals 0, do this:
      Now let number be number divided by 2.
    If not, do this:
      Now let number be 3 times number plus 1.
    Now let steps be steps plus 1.
  Return steps.

For each number from 1 to 10, do this:
  Say number then “ takes ” then hailstone-steps of (number) then “ steps” on a line.
