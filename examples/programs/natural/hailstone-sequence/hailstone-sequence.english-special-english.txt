Note: Hailstone sequence in JavaScript: count the steps to reach 1

Here is how to find hailstone-steps from number:
  Start steps at 0.
  While number is not 1, do this again:
    If number modulo 2 equals 0, do this:
      Change number to number divided by 2.
    If not, do this:
      Change number to 3 times number plus 1.
    Change steps to steps plus 1.
  Return steps.

For each number from 1 to 10, do this:
  Show number then “ takes ” then hailstone-steps of (number) then “ steps” on a line.
