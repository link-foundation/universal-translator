Note: Hailstone sequence in JavaScript: count the steps to reach 1

Rule hailstone-steps with number is defined as follows:
  It is obligatory that steps is initially 0.
  As long as number is not equal to 1 the following applies repeatedly:
    If number modulo 2 is equal to 0 then the following applies:
      It is obligatory that number becomes number divided by 2.
    Otherwise the following applies:
      It is obligatory that number becomes 3 times number plus 1.
    It is obligatory that steps becomes steps plus 1.
  It is obligatory that the result is steps.

For each number from 1 to 10 the following applies:
  It is obligatory that the output shows number followed by “ takes ” followed by hailstone-steps of (number) followed by “ steps” as a line.
