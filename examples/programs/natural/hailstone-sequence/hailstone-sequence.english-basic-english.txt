Note: Hailstone sequence in JavaScript: count the steps to reach 1

Make hailstone-steps from number by these steps:
  Let steps_ be 0.
  While number is not 1, do this again and again:
    If number modulo 2 is 0, do this:
      Let number now be number divided by 2.
    If not, do this:
      Let number now be 3 times number plus 1.
    Let steps_ now be steps_ plus 1.
  Give back steps_.

For every number from 1 till 10, do this:
  Say number then “ takes ” then hailstone-steps of (number) then “ steps” and end the line.
