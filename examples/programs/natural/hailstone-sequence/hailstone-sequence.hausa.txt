Lura: Hailstone sequence in JavaScript: count the steps to reach 1

Ayyana ƙanƙara-matakai da lamba:
  Ƙirƙiri matakai mai ƙima 0.
  Yayin da lamba bai yi daidai da 1, maimaita wannan:
    Idan lamba modulo 2 daidai yake da 0, yi wannan:
      Canza lamba zuwa lamba raba da 2.
    In ba haka ba, yi wannan:
      Canza lamba zuwa 3 sau lamba ƙari 1.
    Canza matakai zuwa matakai ƙari 1.
  Mayar da matakai.

Ga kowane lamba daga 1 zuwa 10, maimaita wannan:
  Rubuta lamba sannan “ takes ” sannan ƙanƙara-matakai na (lamba) sannan “ steps” kuma ka gama layi.
