Note: Sum of squares in JavaScript

Here is how to work out sum-of-squares from limit:
  Let total start as 0.
  Let number start as 1.
  As long as number is at most limit, keep doing this:
    Set total to total plus number times number.
    Set number to number plus 1.
  The answer is total.
