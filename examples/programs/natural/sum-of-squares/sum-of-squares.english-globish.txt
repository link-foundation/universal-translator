Note: Sum of squares in JavaScript

To get sum-of-squares from limit, do this:
  Let total be 0.
  Let number be 1.
  While number is not more than limit, do this again:
    Now let total be total plus number times number.
    Now let number be number plus 1.
  Return total.

Let limit be 10.
Say “Sum of squares from 1 to ” then limit then “:” on a line.
Say sum-of-squares of (limit) on a line.
