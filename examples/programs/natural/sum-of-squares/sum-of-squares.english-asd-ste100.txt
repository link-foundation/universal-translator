Note: Sum of squares in JavaScript

Procedure sum-of-squares with limit:
  Set total to 0.
  Set number to 1.
  While number is not more than limit, do these steps again:
    Change total to total plus number times number.
    Change number to number plus 1.
  Return total.

Set limit to 10.
Write line “Sum of squares from 1 to ” then limit then “:”.
Write line sum-of-squares of (limit).
