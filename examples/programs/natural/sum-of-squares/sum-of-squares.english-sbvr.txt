Note: Sum of squares in JavaScript

Rule sum-of-squares with limit is defined as follows:
  It is obligatory that total is initially 0.
  It is obligatory that number is initially 1.
  As long as number is at most limit the following applies repeatedly:
    It is obligatory that total becomes total plus number times number.
    It is obligatory that number becomes number plus 1.
  It is obligatory that the result is total.

It is obligatory that limit is initially 10.
It is obligatory that the output shows “Sum of squares from 1 to ” followed by limit followed by “:” as a line.
It is obligatory that the output shows sum-of-squares of (limit) as a line.
