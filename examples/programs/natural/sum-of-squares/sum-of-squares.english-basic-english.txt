Note: Sum of squares in JavaScript

Make sum-of-squares from limit by these steps:
  Let total be 0.
  Let number be 1.
  While number is not over limit, do this again and again:
    Let total now be total plus number times number.
    Let number now be number plus 1.
  Give back total.

Let limit be 10.
Say “Sum of squares from 1 to ” then limit then “:” and end the line.
Say sum-of-squares of (limit) and end the line.
