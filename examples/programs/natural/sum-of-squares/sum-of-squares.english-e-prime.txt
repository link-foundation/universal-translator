Note: Sum of squares in JavaScript

Define sum-of-squares using limit:
  Create total with value 0.
  Create number with value 1.
  While number does not exceed limit, repeat this:
    Change total to total plus number times number.
    Change number to number plus 1.
  Give back total.

Create limit with value 10.
Write “Sum of squares from 1 to ” followed by limit followed by “:” and end the line.
Write sum-of-squares of (limit) and end the line.
