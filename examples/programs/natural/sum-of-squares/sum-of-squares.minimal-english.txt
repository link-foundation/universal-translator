Note: Sum of squares in JavaScript

This is how someone does sum-of-squares with limit:
  There is total, it is 0.
  There is number, it is 1.
  When number is not more than limit, do this many times:
    After this, total is total plus number times number.
    After this, number is number plus 1.
  The answer is total.

There is limit, it is 10.
Say “Sum of squares from 1 to ” then limit then “:” on one line.
Say sum-of-squares of (limit) on one line.
