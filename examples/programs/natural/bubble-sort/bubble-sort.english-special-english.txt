Start numbers at the list (5, 3, 8, 1, 9, 2, 7).
Start count at the size of numbers.
For each pass from 0 to count minus 1 minus 1, do this:
  For each index from 0 to count minus 1 minus pass minus 1, do this:
    If part index of numbers is more than part (index plus 1) of numbers, do this:
      Start swap at part index of numbers.
      Change part index of numbers to part (index plus 1) of numbers.
      Change part (index plus 1) of numbers to swap.
For each position from 0 to count minus 1, do this:
  Show part position of numbers on a line.
