Let count be the size of numbers.
For each pass from 0 to count minus 1 minus 1, do this:
  For each index from 0 to count minus 1 minus pass minus 1, do this:

      Let swap be part index of numbers.
      Now let part index of numbers be part (index plus 1) of numbers.
      Now let part (index plus 1) of numbers be swap.
