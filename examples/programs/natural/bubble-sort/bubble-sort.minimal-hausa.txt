Lura: Bubble sort in JavaScript: sort a list of numbers

Akwai adadai, shi ne waɗannan abubuwa (5, 3, 8, 1, 9, 2, 7).
Akwai ƙidaya, shi ne yawan sassan adadai.
Ga kowane zagaye daga 0 zuwa ƙidaya ragi 1 ragi 1, yi wannan:
  Ga kowane fihirisa daga 0 zuwa ƙidaya ragi 1 ragi zagaye ragi 1, yi wannan:
    Idan sashe fihirisa na adadai ya fi sashe (fihirisa ƙari 1) na adadai, yi wannan:
      Akwai musanya, shi ne sashe fihirisa na adadai.
      Bayan wannan, sashe fihirisa na adadai ya zama sashe (fihirisa ƙari 1) na adadai.
      Bayan wannan, sashe (fihirisa ƙari 1) na adadai ya zama musanya.
Ga kowane matsayi daga 0 zuwa ƙidaya ragi 1, yi wannan:
  Faɗi sashe matsayi na adadai a layi ɗaya.
