Note: Bubble sort in JavaScript: sort a list of numbers

Let numbers be the list (5, 3, 8, 1, 9, 2, 7).
Let count be the size of numbers.
For every pass from 0 till count minus 1 minus 1, do this:
  For every index from 0 till count minus 1 minus pass minus 1, do this:
    If part index of numbers is over part (index plus 1) of numbers, do this:
      Let swap be part index of numbers.
      Let part index of numbers now be part (index plus 1) of numbers.
      Let part (index plus 1) of numbers now be swap.
For every position from 0 till count minus 1, do this:
  Say part position of numbers and end the line.
