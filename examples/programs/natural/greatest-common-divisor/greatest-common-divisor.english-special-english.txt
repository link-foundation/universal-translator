Note: Greatest common divisor in JavaScript, using the Euclidean algorithm

Here is how to find greatest-common-divisor from first and second:
  While second is not 0, do this again:
    Start remainder at first modulo second.
    Change first to second.
    Change second to remainder.
  Return first.
