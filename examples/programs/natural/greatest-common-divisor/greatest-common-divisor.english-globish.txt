Note: Greatest common divisor in JavaScript, using the Euclidean algorithm

To get greatest-common-divisor from first and second, do this:
  While second is not 0, do this again:
    Let remainder be first modulo second.
    Now let first be second.
    Now let second be remainder.
  Return first.

Say “The greatest common divisor of 1071 and 462 is ” then greatest-common-divisor of (1071, 462) on a line.
