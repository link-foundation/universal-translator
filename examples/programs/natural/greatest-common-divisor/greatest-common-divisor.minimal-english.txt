Note: Greatest common divisor in JavaScript, using the Euclidean algorithm

This is how someone does greatest-common-divisor with first and second:
  When second is not the same as 0, do this many times:
    There is remainder, it is first modulo second.
    After this, first is second.
    After this, second is remainder.
  The answer is first.

Say “The greatest common divisor of 1071 and 462 is ” then greatest-common-divisor of (1071, 462) on one line.
