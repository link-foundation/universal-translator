Lura: Greatest common divisor in JavaScript, using the Euclidean algorithm

Haka wani yake yin babba-tarayya-rabo da farko da biyu:
  Lokacin da biyu bai yi daidai da 0 ba, yi wannan sau da yawa:
    Akwai saura, shi ne farko modulo biyu.
    Bayan wannan, farko ya zama biyu.
    Bayan wannan, biyu ya zama saura.
  Amsar ita ce farko.

Faɗi “The greatest common divisor of 1071 and 462 is ” sannan babba-tarayya-rabo na (1071, 462) a layi ɗaya.
