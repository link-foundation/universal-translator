Note: Greatest common divisor in JavaScript, using the Euclidean algorithm

Make greatest-common-divisor from first and second by these steps:
  While second is not 0, do this again and again:
    Let remainder be first modulo second.
    Let first now be second.
    Let second now be remainder.
  Give back first.

Say “The greatest common divisor of 1071 and 462 is ” then greatest-common-divisor of (1071, 462) and end the line.
