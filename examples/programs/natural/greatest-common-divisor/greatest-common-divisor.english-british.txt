Note: Greatest common divisor in JavaScript, using the Euclidean algorithm

Define greatest-common-divisor using first and second:
  While second does not equal 0, repeat this:
    Create remainder with value first modulo second.
    Change first to second.
    Change second to remainder.
  Give back first.

Write ‘The greatest common divisor of 1071 and 462 is ’ followed by greatest-common-divisor of (1071, 462) and end the line.
