Note: Greatest common divisor in JavaScript, using the Euclidean algorithm

Rule greatest-common-divisor with first and second is defined as follows:
  As long as second is not equal to 0 the following applies repeatedly:
    It is obligatory that remainder is initially first modulo second.
    It is obligatory that first becomes second.
    It is obligatory that second becomes remainder.
  It is obligatory that the result is first.

It is obligatory that the output shows “The greatest common divisor of 1071 and 462 is ” followed by greatest-common-divisor of (1071, 462) as a line.
